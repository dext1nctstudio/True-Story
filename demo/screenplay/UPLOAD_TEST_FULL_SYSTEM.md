# Full-system upload fixture

Upload `UPLOAD_TEST_FULL_SYSTEM.fountain` through the web interface. The work is
entirely fictional and was written specifically for repeatable system testing.

It deliberately includes:

- true-story framing and recurring named people;
- supported, contradicted, negative, quoted, and unresolved factual claims;
- clearly signposted opinions that should not be researched;
- a music cue, artwork, photograph, tattoo, logo, product, source material,
  film clip, URL/handle, phone number, street address, and vehicle plate;
- conflicting accounts and statements suitable for counsel review;
- multiple scenes and repeated subjects for overlays, rollups, remedies,
  reports, cost telemetry, and live progress events.

Expected results vary by provider and model configuration. In offline/mock mode,
the deterministic extractor has intentionally lower recall, but the fixture
still exercises the complete pipeline shape.
